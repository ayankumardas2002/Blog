This blog doesn't post every day. A new post goes out when it's ready, on the date and time you choose.

## How it works

- Every post is a text file in the `_posts` folder.
- The date at the top of the file decides when it goes live.
- If the date is in the future, the post stays hidden until then.

> Write when you have something to say, and let the schedule handle the timing.
